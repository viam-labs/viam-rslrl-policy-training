# MicroDuck dance: design (milestone 4)

## Goal

Teach the MicroDuck a short in-place dance by imitation, end to end on Viam:

1. choreograph keyframes on a sim machine;
2. record the playback into a Viam sequence dataset;
3. train an imitation policy as a Viam custom training job (mjlab);
4. run it with the duck-policy module on the preview machine and score it against a headless reference.

This milestone is sim-to-sim only, like milestones 1–3. The real duck comes later.

## Decisions (from brainstorming, 2026-11-21)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Dance scope | In place: feet stay planted (knee bob, hip sway, head nods and tilts), one loop of 4–8 s |
| Authoring | Pose keyframes on the sim machine, interpolate, preview |
| Hand-off to training | A Viam sequence dataset of the recorded playback (arm `JointPositions`) |
| Runtime | A separate `dancer` duck-policy service. Only one service drives the duck at a time |
| Training approach | Extend Pollen's phase-pose pattern (crouch, spin, sit-stand): a phase clock in the command slot, pose tracking, the same 61-value observation |

Rejected:
- **mjlab's BeyondMimic motion tracking:** it changes the observation and actions, and nobody has tuned it for the duck.
- **Open-loop playback plus a learned correction:** it would be a different runtime path from walking.

## Background

- **Pollen's phase pattern** (`microduck_rl@0daab16`, `src/mjlab_microduck/tasks/mdp.py`):
  - `GroundPickPhaseCommandCfg(period, randomize_phase)` writes `[cos 2πφ, sin 2πφ, 0]` into the twist slot.
  - `_phase_pose_error` and `phase_pose_track` (Gaussian, `std` 0.3) / `phase_pose_track_l1` reward the joint pose against a target that the phase interpolates.
  - The command slot layout is `[twist(3), head_pose(4), body_pose(6)]`, 13 values.
- **Limb arms on a BAM robot are read-only.** A floating duck tips over in about 1 s without a policy (milestone 1). Posing therefore needs a separate pinned robot.
- **Sequence datasets:**
  - A sequence is a time window over captured data on one part: the resources and methods to include (for example an arm's `JointPositions`), plus tags.
  - A custom training job on a sequence dataset gets `--binary_data_file`, `--tabular_data_file` and `--sequences_file` (Parquet), and no `--dataset_file`.
  - `viam dataset export` writes the same files for local testing.

## Part 1: choreographing and recording

### Pinned duck

- **`duck_pinned.xml`, added to the duck-sim package** (the next version, 0.3.0):
  - generated by `scripts/export_duck_scene.py` from the same source as `duck.xml`;
  - the free joint removed, so the trunk is fixed at the STAND keyframe's height;
  - plain position actuators (`kp` taken from the walk model), with no BAM.
- **The machine is `microduck-choreo` in DevRelDemos,** a new machine, created only after confirming with the user. The walking preview machine is left alone. Its config, `docs/superpowers/specs/duck-choreo-machine.json`, holds:
  - mujoco-sim as a local module (the same floating-base worktree as the preview machine);
  - robot `duck` with `mjcf: ${packages.archive.duck-sim}/duck_pinned.xml` and no `actuators` attribute;
  - the arms `left-leg`, `head` and `right-leg` with `home_keyframe: "STAND"`. They are writable here (`MoveToJointPositions`);
  - an `overview` camera;
  - the data manager service, with capture on each arm's `JointPositions` at 50 Hz and sync on.

### Dance file

`dances/<name>.json` is the source of truth, kept in git:

```json
{
  "name": "bob-sway",
  "period_s": 6.0,
  "keyframes": [
    {"t": 0.0,  "pose": {}},
    {"t": 0.25, "pose": {"left_knee": -0.9, "right_knee": 0.9, "head_roll": 0.2}},
    {"t": 0.5,  "pose": {}},
    {"t": 0.75, "pose": {"left_knee": -0.9, "right_knee": 0.9, "head_roll": -0.2}}
  ]
}
```

(The joint names are illustrative; the real ones come from the walk model.)

- `t` runs from 0 to 1 and must be strictly increasing, starting at 0.
- A joint a keyframe doesn't list is at STAND.
- The loop wraps: interpolation runs from the last keyframe back to the first at t = 1.
- **Interpolation:** periodic cubic (Catmull-Rom over the wrapped keyframes), per joint, clipped to the joint limits.
- **Pose limit:** the keyframe at t = 0 must be STAND within 0.05 rad on every joint. Phase 0 is the start pose and the hold pose (Part 3).

### `scripts/duck_choreo.py`

| Subcommand | What it does |
|---|---|
| `pose <dance> --t <0..1>` | Reads the three arms' joint positions from the choreo machine and inserts or replaces the keyframe at `t`. Joints within 0.01 rad of STAND are left out. |
| `preview <dance> [--cycles N]` | Plays the interpolated loop on the pinned duck at 50 Hz through the arms. |
| `check <dance>` | Offline foot check, described below. Exits non-zero on failure. |
| `record <dance> [--cycles 3]` | Runs `check` first. Then it previews N cycles with capture on, waits for sync, creates a sequence over the window (the three arms' `JointPositions`, tags `dance:<name>` and `period:<period_s>`), and adds it to the `microduck-dances` sequence dataset, creating the dataset if it's missing. |

`interpolate(dance, phase) -> 14 joint angles` (policy order) lives in `duck_train/dance.py`, so the tool, the reference builder's tests and the evaluator share one implementation.

**Foot check.** Pinning the trunk hides foot motion, so the check samples 200 phases. At each one it:
1. sets the pose in the floating `duck.xml` with physics off (`mj_kinematics`);
2. moves the trunk vertically so the lower foot site (`left_foot` / `right_foot`) touches z = 0.

It fails if:
- the other foot is more than 1 cm above the floor at any phase;
- either foot's horizontal position, relative to the trunk at the trunk height, moves more than 2 cm over the loop;
- the interpolated pose leaves the joint limits.

## Part 2: training

### Job inputs

- **`microduck-train` gains `task=velocity|dance`,** defaulting to `velocity`. A velocity job is unchanged.
- **A dance job:**
  - is submitted on the `microduck-dances` dataset, so it receives the three Parquet files;
  - takes `dance=<name>`, which is required;
  - has defaults `max_iterations=2000`, `num_envs=4096`, `save_interval=250` and `video_every_iters=500`;
  - uses the same video upload arguments as velocity.

### Reference builder (`duck_train/dance.py`)

`build_reference(binary, tabular, sequences, dance) -> (period_s, table[100, 14])`:

1. Select the sequences tagged `dance:<name>`. There must be at least one. Read `period:<s>` from the tag, and require every selected sequence to have the same period.
2. From the tabular Parquet, take each sequence's `JointPositions` rows for the three arms, and convert degrees to radians (Viam arms report degrees).
3. Order the joints by the arm joint order: left leg ×5, head ×4, right leg ×5, which is the policy order.
4. Put the three arms on a common time base by linear interpolation at 50 Hz.
5. Drop the first and last 0.25 s of each window. Find the start of a cycle as the sample closest to STAND, so phase 0 is STAND.
6. Fold the samples into 100 phase bins by `period`, and take the mean of each bin over every cycle in every sequence.
7. Fail if any bin is empty, if the per-bin spread (the standard deviation across cycles) is over 0.05 rad, or if bin 0 differs from STAND by more than 0.05 rad.

The table is used by the training task and written to the package as `reference.json`.

### Task `Mjlab-Dance-Flat-MicroDuck` (`duck_train/dance_task.py`)

It's registered at job start and built from Pollen's legged sit-stand config (`microduck_sitstand_env_cfg`), changed as follows.

- **Command `twist`:** `GroundPickPhaseCommandCfg(period=period_s, randomize_phase=True)`.
  - **20 % of envs get a frozen phase:** φ = 0, command `[1, 0, 0]`. This is the stand-and-hold case.
  - It's done with a small subclass, `DancePhaseCommand`, that chooses frozen envs at reset with `rel_frozen_envs = 0.2`.
- **Command `head_pose`:** a constant zero, keeping the slot layout. The dance drives the head through the reference.
- **Rewards** (all of the sit-stand posture and height terms removed):

  | Term | Function | Weight (initial) |
  |---|---|---|
  | `dance_pose` | Gaussian (std 0.3) on all 14 joints vs `table[φ]`, linearly interpolated between bins | 4.0 |
  | `dance_pose_l1` | −mean abs error vs `table[φ]` | 1.0 |
  | `upright` | Pollen's velocity-task upright term | velocity value |
  | `foot_slip` | velocity-task term | velocity value |
  | `feet_contact` | +1 when both feet are in contact | 0.5 |
  | `action_rate_l2` | mjlab | −0.1 |
  | `dof_pos_limits`, `self_collisions`, `body_ang_vel` | velocity-task terms | velocity values |

  `dance_pose` and `dance_pose_l1` are new functions in `duck_train/dance_task.py`. They take the phase from the command the same way `_phase_pose_error` does.
- **Terminations:** `fell_over` is kept (sit-stand removes it, but falls aren't part of an in-place dance), plus `time_out` at 20 s and `nan_state`.
- **Randomization:** the velocity task's domain randomization, the BAM actuator, encoder bias and pushes, as configured in `microduck_velocity_env_cfg`.
- **Curriculum:** none at first. If the probe fails to learn, the first thing to add is a ramp from STAND to the full reference.

### Package `microduck-dance-<name>`

- `policy.onnx`, checked with `verify_onnx`.
- `config.json`: the existing schema, plus `"command": {"mode": "phase", "period_s": <s>}`, which replaces `twist_min` and `twist_max`. `safety.action_bound` comes from the training stats, as for velocity.
- `reference.json`: `{"period_s", "joints", "table"}`.
- `model_metrics.json`.

`duck_train.package.build_config` gains the phase variant. A velocity package is unchanged.

## Part 3: running it and evaluating

### Phase mode in duck-policy

When `config.json` has `command.mode == "phase"`:

- Each tick, φ = ((t − t_start) / period_s) mod 1, and the command is `[cos 2πφ, sin 2πφ, 0, …0]`, 13 values. While holding, φ is frozen at 0.
- `start {"cycles": N}` (default 0, meaning forever) starts at φ = 0. After N full cycles it holds at φ = 0 and keeps running.
- `set_command` returns an error in phase mode.
- `status` adds `phase`, `cycles_done` and `holding`.
- The safety limits and the stop reasons are unchanged.

`Command` and `build_obs` stay as they are. The phase code makes the 13-value command vector itself.

### Preview machine

- `dancer` is a second `devrel:duck-policy:controller` service, with `duck`, the `dance-model` onnx-cpu service and `package_dir` pointing at `microduck-dance-<name>`.
- It's added to `docs/superpowers/specs/duck-sim-machine.json` next to `walker`.
- **Switching:** the caller stops one service at a stand and starts the other. No new code; it's documented and exercised by the evaluation script.

### Evaluation

- **Reference:** `scripts/duck_reference.py` gains `--phase <period_s>`. It drives the command from the phase clock instead of the script, runs our dance ONNX in Pollen's headless setup, and runs 3 cycles plus a 2 s hold.
- **Ours:** `scripts/duck_parity.py ours --phase`:
  - runs 20 episodes of the same schedule through `dancer`;
  - records the joint positions (from `io`), the trunk pose and the foot sites at 10 Hz;
  - records the script/phase settings in its output.
- **Report:** `scripts/duck_parity.py report` checks:

  | Check | Pass |
  |---|---|
  | slip-free episodes | ≥ 18/20 |
  | falls | ≤ reference falls + 1 |
  | joint RMSE vs `reference.json` | ours ≤ 1.25 × the reference runner's |
  | foot drift | < 3 cm per cycle (each foot, horizontal) |
  | trunk yaw drift | < 10° per episode |
  | tick rate / p99 / late ticks / walker ran | same thresholds as milestone 2 |
  | hold | upright and walker running at the end of the 2 s hold |

- **Handover check:** one scripted run of walker (stand 2 s), then dancer (2 cycles), then walker (stand 2 s), with no fall.
- **Results** go in `docs/superpowers/specs/<date>-microduck-dance-results.md`.

## Testing

Unit tests (pytest, no GPU and no Viam):

- **`interpolate`:** hits each keyframe exactly, wraps continuously at t = 1, missing joints are STAND, the output is clipped to the limits, and a bad `t` order is rejected.
- **Foot check:** passes a dance that's only head motion, and fails a dance that lifts one knee (synthetic).
- **`build_reference`:** on synthetic Parquet built from a known dance, with three cycles, degrees and some jitter. The table matches `interpolate` within 0.01 rad. It fails on a missing tag, mixed periods or a large spread.
- **Phase mode in the loop:** the command vector at known times, the hold after N cycles, and `set_command` rejected.
- **`build_config`:** the phase variant.

**Probe:** before the full run, a 200-iteration dance job checks the path end to end: the dataset in, the task registered, the package written and `verify_onnx` passing.

## Build order

1. **Choreographing and recording:** pinned duck in duck-sim 0.3.0, `duck_train/dance.py` `interpolate` plus the foot check, `duck_choreo.py`, the choreo machine, and a first dance recorded into `microduck-dances`.
2. **Training:** `build_reference`, the task, `task=dance` in `microduck-train` (a new script version), the probe, then the full run.
3. **Running and evaluating:** phase mode in duck-policy, the `dancer` service, reference and parity `--phase`, the handover check, and the results doc.

## Out of scope

- Stepping or weight-shift dances.
- One service that switches between policies itself.
- The real duck.
- A curriculum, unless the probe shows it's needed.
- Editing dances in a UI. Keyframes are edited through `duck_choreo.py pose` and the JSON file.
